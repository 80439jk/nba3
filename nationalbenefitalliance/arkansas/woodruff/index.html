Utility Assistance

Help paying electric, gas, and water bills in Woodruff

LIHEAP — Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program

Federal program providing energy bill assistance and crisis intervention for eligible low-income Woodruff residents. Covers electric, gas, propane, and cooling costs. Administered by Arkansas Department of Human Services (DHS).

📞 1-800-4-ENERGY 🌐 LIHEAP National Hotline
Application Period 🗓️ Seasonal
Oct 1 – Aug 15 (or until funds exhausted)
Priority: Seniors 60+, disabled, families w/ young children, disconnected utilities.
Eligibility Household income ≤ 60% of State Median Income
1 person: ~$2,600/mo · 4 people: ~$5,000/mo
Documents Needed
  • Proof of income (last 30 days)
  • Recent utility bill
  • Social Security numbers for household
  • Photo ID for head of household
How to Apply
📞 State LIHEAP Office 📞 Dial 211 🏢 Local CAA Office
  1. Gather documents: proof of income (last 30 days), recent utility bill, Social Security numbers, photo ID
  2. Contact your state LIHEAP office, call 211, or visit your local Community Action Agency
  3. Complete application intake interview (in-person, phone, or online)
  4. Receive eligibility determination (processing typically 30-45 days during season)
  5. Once approved, funds are sent directly to your utility company

Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers

Federally-funded rental subsidy vouchers covering the difference between 30% of your income and fair market rent. Administered locally by your county housing authority.

📞 Call 211 🌐 HUD Section 8 Info
Waitlist Status 🔒 Often Closed
Many areas have long waitlists that are frequently closed. Check your local housing authority website for current status.
Eligibility Household income ≤ 50% of Area Median Income (AMI)
1 person: ~$30,000–35,000/yr
4 people: ~$45,000–50,000/yr (varies)
How to Apply Contact your local county/city housing authority to see if they're accepting applications or maintaining a waitlist. Even if closed, sign up to be notified when reopened.

Food Assistance

SNAP, food pantries, and nutrition programs in Woodruff

SNAP (Food Stamps)

SNAP (Food Assistance) providing monthly EBT food benefits for low-income Woodruff residents. Purchase food at authorized retailers. Faster processing through online applications.

📞 1-800-SNAP (7627) 🌐 Your State SNAP Portal
Application Period 🔄 Rolling
Apply anytime. Standard: 30 days. Expedited: 5 days if eligible.
Eligibility Gross income ≤ 130% Federal Poverty Level (most states; some go to 165%)
1 person: ~$1,580/mo (130% FPL to $2,430/mo at 200% FPL)
4 people: ~$3,250/mo (130% FPL to $5,000/mo at 200% FPL)
No asset test in most states. Rates updated annually.
Documents Needed
  • Photo ID
  • Social Security numbers
  • Proof of income (pay stubs, SSA letter)
  • Proof of housing costs (rent/utilities)
How to Apply
🌐 Online (Fastest) 📞 Phone 🏢 Local Office
  1. Gather documents: photo ID, Social Security numbers, proof of income (pay stubs, SSA letter), proof of housing costs (rent/utilities)
  2. Apply online through your state's SNAP portal, call 1-800-SNAP, or visit your local SNAP office
  3. Complete an eligibility interview (may be done by phone)
  4. Receive eligibility determination: standard 30 days or expedited 5 days if eligible
  5. Once approved, receive EBT card in the mail and start shopping for food at authorized retailers

WIC — Women, Infants & Children Nutrition Program

Supplemental nutrition for pregnant women, new mothers, infants, and children up to age 5. Provides vouchers for milk, eggs, fruits/vegetables, cereal, and infant formula, plus nutrition education and healthcare referrals.

📞 1-800-WIC-BREASTFEEDING 🌐 WIC Program Info
Who Qualifies 🔄 Rolling
Pregnant, breastfeeding, or postpartum women; infants and children ≤ 5. Income ≤ 185% FPL. Auto-eligible if on Medicaid/SNAP.
Documents Needed

  • Proof of income or benefit card

  • Proof of pregnancy or child's birth certificate

Frequently Asked Questions About Woodruff Resources

How do I apply for SNAP in Arkansas?
Contact your local SNAP (Food Assistance) office or visit the state benefits portal. You'll need photo ID, Social Security numbers, proof of income, and proof of housing costs. Processing typically takes 30 days, or 5 days for expedited cases.
What is LIHEAP and how do I apply in Woodruff?
LIHEAP (Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program) provides federal energy bill assistance. Administered by Arkansas Department of Human Services (DHS). You must meet income limits (typically 60% of State Median Income) and provide proof of income and utility bills. Visit the state's benefits portal or call 211 for local application help.
Where can I find free food assistance in Woodruff?
Apply for SNAP food stamps through SNAP (Food Assistance), visit local food banks and food pantries (use 211.org to locate), or contact faith-based organizations in your area. Most food pantries are walk-in with no appointment or application required.
What are the income limits for Arkansas Medicaid in Arkansas?
Income limits vary by family size and eligibility category. Generally, adults qualify if their income is at or below 138% of the Federal Poverty Level. Children and pregnant women may have higher income thresholds. Contact your local Arkansas Department of Human Services for specific limits for your household.
How do I file for unemployment benefits in Arkansas?
Visit the state unemployment office website (https://www.arkansas.gov/dws) to file online, or apply by phone. You'll need Social Security number, driver's license, and information about your job loss. Filing early is important—benefits are based on the date you file, not the date you lost your job.
What is the 211 service and how do I use it?
211 is a free, confidential referral service that connects you to local resources for housing, food, health care, employment, and other assistance. Simply dial 211 from any phone (no area code needed) or visit 211.org to search online for services in your area.
Can I get help paying my utility bills if I'm low-income?
Yes! LIHEAP provides federal energy bill assistance for households at or below 60% of State Median Income. Apply in fall (October/November) as funding is seasonal and often runs out. Other utility companies also offer assistance programs—call your provider or dial 211 to ask.
How much in benefits can I get from SNAP (food stamps)?
SNAP benefits depend on your household size and income. The maximum monthly benefit in 2026 is about $291 per person for a single-person household, but eligible households typically receive less based on income. Visit your state's SNAP office or online portal to estimate your benefits.
Are there housing assistance programs for renters in Woodruff?
Yes! Emergency Rental Assistance helps people facing eviction, Section 8 provides rental vouchers for low-income families, and faith-based organizations offer emergency shelter and transitional housing. Dial 211 to find available programs and waitlist information in your area.
What documents do I need to apply for benefits in Woodruff?
Most benefit applications require: photo ID, Social Security numbers, proof of income (pay stubs, SSA letters), proof of housing costs (rent/utility bills), and proof of residency. Some programs may ask for birth certificates or proof of citizenship. Check with the specific program or call 211 for detailed requirements.
